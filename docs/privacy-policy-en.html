  • Clearing browser data or removing the extension deletes everything you saved.

In accordance with Article 30 of the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea, the Operator publishes this policy to protect personal information and to handle related complaints promptly.

Article 1 (Purpose of Processing and Whether Data Is Collected)

The Operator does not collect your personal information while providing the Service, and does not use it or provide it to third parties. Collection purposes, collected items and retention periods are therefore not applicable.

Article 2 (Information Stored in Your Browser)

To provide its features, the Service stores the information below only in the browser installed on your device (Chrome extension storage). It is never sent to the Operator, and the Operator cannot access it.

Stored informationPurposeLocation / period
Sentences and images you chose to saveBrowsing, copying and deleting saved itemsYour browser / until you delete them or remove the extension
Saved page URL, site name, save time, preview-image addressShowing the source, opening the original page, grouping by site and dateSame as above
Settings such as display modeKeeping your popup / new window / side panel choiceSame as above
  • Saved content is whatever you select on a web page. Whether it contains anyone’s personal information depends on the original text you select. The Operator does not view or analyze it.
  • The Service reads a page’s preview-image address only in the tab where you triggered the save. It does not read other tabs or your browsing history.
  • Thumbnails shown in the list are loaded directly from the original website by your browser. This follows that website’s policy, and the Operator is not involved.

Article 3 (Retention Period and Deletion)

  1. The Operator holds no personal information, so there is no separate deletion procedure.
  2. You can delete data saved in your browser yourself by:
    • using the delete function on each card in the Service
    • clearing site data / extension data in Chrome
    • removing the extension (saved data is deleted with it)
  3. Data saved in the browser remains until you delete it.

Article 4 (Provision to Third Parties)

The Operator does not provide your personal information to third parties. Saved data is not sold, and is not transmitted or transferred for advertising, credit decisions or any other purpose.

Article 5 (Outsourcing of Processing)

The Operator does not outsource the processing of personal information. The Service uses no separate server, cloud or database.

Article 6 (Transfer Abroad)

The Operator does not transmit or store personal information in any country or region, including Korea, so there is no transfer abroad.

Article 7 (Automatic Collection Tools — Cookies)

The Service installs and operates no cookies and uses no advertising identifiers, visit analytics or behavioral tracking. It uses only Chrome extension storage.

Article 8 (Children Under 14, Sensitive and Unique Identifiers)

There is no sign-up and no collection of information, so the Operator does not collect children’s personal information, sensitive information or unique identifiers.

Article 9 (Automated Decisions and Generative AI)

The Service makes no automated decisions using artificial intelligence, does not use your saved content to train AI, and does not send it to external AI services.

Article 10 (Purpose of Extension Permissions)

The Service requests no host permissions and does not download or run remote code. Information obtained through these permissions is not used for any other purpose.

Article 11 (Rights of Data Subjects and How to Exercise Them)

You have the right to request access, correction, deletion and suspension of processing of your personal information. All data of the Service is in your browser, so you can check, copy and delete it directly in the Service. The Operator does not hold the data and cannot view or delete it on your behalf. For other inquiries, use the contact in Article 13.

Rights under privacy laws that may apply to users in other jurisdictions (such as GDPR in the EEA/UK or CCPA in California) can likewise be exercised by you directly in the browser. The Operator does not process personal data covered by those laws through this Service.

Article 12 (Security Measures)

The Operator runs no server or database that collects or stores personal information, so there is no personal information subject to separate security measures. The Service is built on these principles:

  • Saved data is never transmitted outside your device.
  • Only the minimum necessary permissions are requested.
  • No external scripts, advertising or analytics tools are included.

Security of your device and browser account (screen lock, account protection, browser sync settings) is your responsibility. If you turn on Chrome sync, extension data may be synced through your Google account under Google’s policies, which is a separate area the Operator cannot access.
